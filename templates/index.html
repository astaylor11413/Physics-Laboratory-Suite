Phase 1: Tube Core Cut & Metric Finish Measure and mark the 2-foot PVC pipe (1/2-inch inner diameter) using your pen or pencil and metric ruler or meter stick. Clean raw edges using sandpaper.
Phase 2: Wrap Clearance Sleeve Component Secure the 3" x 5" Index card firmly around the structural PVC tube core, securing structural seams with strips of electrical tape.
Phase 3: Size and Fit Exterior Spool Guide Walls Use scissors to trim the 5" x 8" Index card to form a guided exterior frame spool structure.
Phase 4: Helix Wire Spool Winding Wind tight turns of the 28 AWG enameled magnet wire around the card spool. Use sandpaper to sand down the terminal endpoints to clear the insulating enamel.
Phase 5: Secure Final Shell Jacket Seal Wrap your coil configuration with clear packaging tape. (Optional: Use a drill if anchor terminal mount placements are preferred).
